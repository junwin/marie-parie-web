From the owner

I first opened for business in 1996. The shop has changed shape a few times in the past 11 years. Originally we were called Rock Bottom and sold music related items as well as vintage clothing. Four years ago I decided to follow my passion for hand made creations and French couture and created Marie Parie. Through the years and transitions I've enjoyed buying items of interest and whimsy, supporting artists, exploring my own creativity and building relationships with my customers.

A candid interview with Marie Parie

Where do you hail from?
Paris, France
Favorite Color
Pink, pink and more pink!
Special Interest
Yoga, para-sailing, hoola hooping!
Favorite Pastimes
Shopping with gal pals at the Paris Flea Market, sipping cappuccino on a chilly night, hanging out with my precious puppy: BUTTON

What brought you here?

Do you miss Paris?
No — because I jet to Paris on all of those buying trips!
What is your personal philosophy?
Enjoy every moment. Laugh with friends. Shop till you drop!
