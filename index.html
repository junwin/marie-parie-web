Meet the Proprietor ♡

Bonjour, lovelies!

For 30 years, I’ve had the pleasure of doing what I love—creating, collecting, styling, and finding those wonderfully unusual little things that make life more beautiful.

I believe in wearing what makes you happy, surrounding yourself with things you adore, and never being afraid of a little whimsy. Life is far too short for boring clothes, bare walls, or taking oneself too seriously.

Come say bonjour!
